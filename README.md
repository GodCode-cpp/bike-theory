# Motorbike Theory Test Practice - Botswana (Class A, A1, A2)

A free practice app for the Botswana **motorcycle learner's licence theory test** (Class A2, A1 and A). 168 questions, mock tests and road sign questions. It works **offline** on iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows, with no sign-up and no ads.

**Open it now: https://godcode-cpp.github.io/bike-theory/**

## What's inside

- **168 questions**: 150 on the 8 lessons of the handbook (clothing and fitness, road safety and licence classes, gears and braking, critical situations and signs, being seen, other road users, sidecars and accidents, yard test exercises) plus 18 road sign questions.
- **Exam style**: answer every question, see your score at the end. Pass mark is 80%, and you can go back and change answers before you finish.
- **Practice mode**: instant feedback with a short explanation after each answer.
- **Mock test**: 30 mixed questions including 5 road signs.
- **Review mistakes**, **shuffle everything**, **study cards**, and one tile per lesson.
- Wrong answers are written to look believable, so you have to know the answer rather than spot the odd one out.
- Your progress is saved on your own device. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere.

## Install it on your phone (works offline afterwards)

Open the link above once while you have internet, then add it to your home screen.

**iPhone / iPad** (use Safari)
1. Open https://godcode-cpp.github.io/bike-theory/ in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open the app from its icon once and wait for the home screen to load.
4. After that it works with no signal, even in Airplane Mode.

**Android** (use Chrome)
1. Open the link in Chrome.
2. Tap the **three dots** menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Open it from the new icon once while online.

## Install it on a computer

- **Chrome or Edge (Windows, Mac):** open the link, then click the **install** icon at the right of the address bar.
- **Safari on a Mac:** open the link, then **File > Add to Dock**.
- Or just keep the page open or bookmarked. After the first visit it also loads offline in the same browser.

## Download a single file instead

Prefer a file you can keep? Download **[Motorbike-Theory-Class-A.html](https://github.com/GodCode-cpp/bike-theory/releases/latest/download/Motorbike-Theory-Class-A.html)** and double-click it. It opens in your browser and needs no internet. (On an iPhone, the Home Screen method above is more reliable than opening a downloaded file.)

Progress is stored per browser and per device, so your phone and your computer keep separate scores.

## Good to know

- This is an **unofficial study aid**. It is not made by, or endorsed by, the Botswana Department of Road Transport and Safety (DRTS).
- The questions are based on the *Syllabus (Handbook) for Motorbike - Class (A2) A1 and A*, Department of Road Transport and Safety, Botswana, First Edition 2005 (ISBN 99912-548-0-3). That handbook is copyright of its owners and is not included here.
- Rules, signs and the real test format can change. Always check the current requirements with DRTS or your driving school. Passing the practice tests does not guarantee passing the real test.
